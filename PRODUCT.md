# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary (inferred from bio and OJT goals):** OJT / internship supervisors, technical leads, and hiring managers evaluating Ryan's full-stack web engineering ability, real-world shipping record, and readiness to join a team.
- **Secondary:** Local businesses, municipal offices (LGUs), and campus organizations in Camiguin and Region 10 reviewing Ryan's shipped systems and competition record.

## Product Purpose

Showcase Ryan Marc L. Salon's shipped software systems, defended IoT + web capstone, technical stack, and regional competition wins so visitors can quickly evaluate his engineering craft, inspect his work without friction, and reach out for OJT / internship opportunities or project collaborations.

## Positioning

A 4th-year BSIT student from Mahinog, Camiguin who doesn't just build toy tutorials — he ships real civic, healthcare, LGU, campus, and island-business web systems (including a defended IoT + web health kiosk capstone) and competes regionally across startup pitching and cybersecurity CTFs.

## Operating Context

Visitors scan the portfolio on laptops and mobile phones during workdays or evening review sessions. Comfort during sustained reading of project summaries, stack details, and competition stories is essential.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 18 + Vite + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS + GSAP + Lenis single-page application with hash-based routing (`#` home + `#/achievements` dedicated Competitions & Achievements page).
- Must support both an **eye-friendly, low-glare default theme** and a clean theme toggle (light & dark both soft and restful, never harsh `#000000` / `#ffffff` glare).
- External live demo and GitHub repository URLs for individual projects are intentionally left optional (`liveUrl?`, `repoUrl?`) for Ryan to add later; projects must still be meaningfully inspectable via clean screenshot previews and descriptions.
- Preserve all factual claims strictly — never invent awards, clients, testimonials, or links.

## Product Principles

1. **Calm, eye-friendly reading first:** Low-glare tinted surfaces, generous whitespace, and clear typographic hierarchy beat theatrical dark-mode effects.
2. **Real shipped proof leads:** Surface Ryan's 6 real Camiguin/CPSC/LGU systems and regional wins immediately instead of hiding them behind gimmicks.
3. **Honest & grounded:** Let real screenshots, competition photos, and plain language speak — zero agency clichés.
4. **Effortless exploration:** Instant first paint, clear navigation between Home and Achievements, accessible keyboard/mobile interactions, and crisp image previews.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA contrast (≥4.5:1 body text, ≥3:1 large text/controls) across both light and dark modes.
- Full keyboard navigation, visible `:focus-visible` indicators, semantic landmarks, `44×44px` minimum mobile touch targets, and `prefers-reduced-motion` compliance.
